The same pork carried three published prices in one Chinese city in February 2026: 8.14 yuan per 500 grams wholesale, 14.07 at the farm market, 15.03 at a chain supermarket counter. Vegetables that month ran 1.55 wholesale, 3.94 at a community vegetable shop and 4.52 in a supermarket, with no farm-market column published at all ([a municipal development and reform commission bulletin](https://fgw.beijing.gov.cn/gzdt/fgzs/gzdt/202603/t20260317_4559408.htm), 2026-03-17).

That is the useful resolution. A single "China food price" averages five shops that are not substitutes, and the wholesale-to-retail gap is wider than the gap between retail channels.

Below: unit prices by channel where a source exists, three baskets, and why none of this is one figure.

## The five channels

**Early market (早市).** Opens before dawn, gone by late morning, often selling produce that passed through wholesale hours earlier. In one district of a city in eastern China, thirty years of unlicensed roadside early markets were moved onto a managed lot that now runs around the clock: wholesale from about 01:00, retail through the day, a night market from the evening ([Zhejiang Online](https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202608/t20260824_31865584.shtml), 2026-08-24).

**Wet market (菜市场).** The permanent covered market, and the channel the official price system actually samples: the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs monitors 500 county-level farm markets and collection points for its weekly livestock and egg series ([MARA, 2026-01-13](https://xmsyj.moa.gov.cn/jcyj/202601/t20260113_6480632.htm)).

**Neighbourhood and chain supermarkets.** Two prices, not one. The February bulletin separates a community vegetable shop from a chain supermarket counter: 3.94 against 4.52 for vegetables, 14.07 against 15.03 for pork.

**Warehouse clubs.** Membership retail. Sam's Club's two tiers were 260 and 680 yuan a year, with 10.7 million paid members in China as of June 2026 ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/companies/article/3366091/walmarts-sams-club-accelerates-expansion-china-membership-passes-107-million), 2026-09-02). Costco added a 688-yuan black-diamond tier paying a 3 percent rebate in April 2026, against a 299-yuan basic tier ([Lianshang, via Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-04-22/doc-inhvkiya9379250.shtml), 2026-04-22). Neither retailer's membership page would load, so both are reported, not confirmed.

**Fresh-grocery delivery.** Pre-warehouse apps, about 30 minutes to your door. What is documented is the fees, not the item prices: one pilot charges 0.6 yuan base packaging plus 0.4 for insulated items, a second 0.5 plus 0.5, others 1.0 or 1.2 flat ([Lanjing Finance, via East Money](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608193846383530.html), 2026-08-19). One platform suspended self-pickup in several cities, some surviving pickup points added a 20-yuan minimum, and most cities charge 3 to 5 yuan below a free-delivery threshold of 35 to 39 yuan ([Jiemian, via Securities Times](https://stcn.com/article/detail/3822368.html), 2026-04-27).

The first five columns are one city's February 2026 bulletin, the only public format I found that prints wholesale, farm-market and supermarket prices side by side ([link](https://fgw.beijing.gov.cn/gzdt/fgzs/gzdt/202603/t20260317_4559408.htm), 2026-03-17). Two cells are weaker than they look. The vegetable farm-market figure is a 20-variety average in one city in May, not a February stall price — the February bulletin carries no farm-market column for vegetables. And the second city's pork average covers every cut it monitors, which pulls it below the mixed-cut price beside it.

Same bulletin, the staples: japonica rice 3.25 at the farm market against 2.93 at the supermarket, plain flour 2.21 against 2.23, blended oil 67.05 against 73.85 a 5-litre bottle, peanut oil 150.33 against 160.26 ([link](https://fgw.beijing.gov.cn/gzdt/fgzs/gzdt/202603/t20260317_4559408.htm), 2026-03-17).

## Three monthly baskets

My baskets, not anyone's survey: the quantities are an assumption; every unit price behind them is cited above. One person, one month, prices per kilogram.

| Pork | 3 kg | 28.14 | 30.06 | 30.06 |
| Beef | 1 kg | 69.54 | 84.94 | 69.54 |
| Eggs | 1.5 kg | 8.82 | 9.80 | 9.80 |

| Rice | 4 kg | 6.50 | 5.86 | 5.86 |

| **Total** | | **¥335** | **¥427** | **¥565** |

Localised buys vegetables and meat at a farm market, rice at the farm-market price, and nothing for packaging, because it does not order online. Mixed buys vegetables, meat and eggs at chain-supermarket counters, fruit at the contributor-reported average, and pays 1.00 yuan an order on 12 delivery orders a month — the middle of the four reported fees, and my order count, not a measured one. Imported-heavy keeps domestic beef, since I could not verify a 2026 imported price; buys two of its five fruit kilograms as one shipped durian at the price a live-stream shopper pays; and adds half a kilogram of "local" cheese, the nearest public price that might be foreign.

The gap between the first two baskets is 27 percent, mostly vegetables: retail vegetables start at nearly three times their wholesale price, pork at under twice. The fruit row is the weakest number here: I found no channel-differentiated retail fruit series, so two baskets share one proxy.

## Time and language, which are also prices

The channel open at 19:00 is the channel that closes at midday.

| | Early / wet market | Supermarket | Warehouse club | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Window | before dawn to midday; permanent markets run all day | long retail hours, all week | store hours; one location, often a long trip | about 30 minutes, to your door |
| Serving | per counter, per item; you are served | self-service | self-service, one till queue | none |
| Price posted as | spoken, per 斤 | labelled | labelled | labelled |
| Language needed | speaking; cut and vegetable names are local | none to minimal | minimal to none | none |
| Payment | QR code; a foreign card works inside Alipay or WeChat Pay | card or QR | membership card, sold on site | app account, Chinese number in practice |
| The awkward part | the price is written nowhere you can read from a distance | what is left at that hour | the pack size sets your unit price | no item price is in any published series |

The 2025 EF English Proficiency Index puts China 86th of 123 countries and regions, score 464 and speaking 448, against a global average of 488 ([EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/regions/asia/china)). Shopping is exactly when the speaking sub-score is what you are using.

On payment the limits are documented: a foreign card linked to Alipay or WeChat Pay has a ceiling of 5,000 US dollars per transaction and 50,000 a year ([a municipal government service page](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-PaymentMethods/20240313/6f4e58272f1a4cea9aec59c518915bdf.html), published March 2024, updated 2026-07-24). Transactions at or below 200 yuan carry no fee, anything above carries 3 percent — WeChat Pay's own FAQ, reproduced by a provincial government page ([link](http://www.qb.gd.gov.cn/qsxw/content/post_1118497.html), 2023-07-22) and restated in a fee-waiver announcement ([CNR](https://tech.cnr.cn/techph/20250526/t20250526_527185715.shtml), 2025-05-26). Neither company's own fee page would load.

## Why these numbers can only be ranges

**Region.** On collection day 2026-01-08 the ministry's 500-county monitoring put pork at 26.27 yuan per kilo in South China and 19.38 in the Northeast — 36 percent apart, same day, same dataset ([MARA](https://xmsyj.moa.gov.cn/jcyj/202601/t20260113_6480632.htm), 2026-01-13). On 2026-07-23 the same series had 24.16 against 18.06, still 34 percent apart ([week 4 of July](https://xmsyj.moa.gov.cn/jcyj/202607/t20260729_6486285.htm), 2026-07-29). No national average survives that.

**Season.** The 28-variety wholesale basket averaged 5.77 yuan per kilo in the week of 22–28 December 2025 ([Farmers' Daily via Xinhua](http://www.news.cn/food/20260106/b1c09adc0f2a40389df51c51db781034/c.html), 2026-01-06) and 4.21 in the week of 13–19 July 2026 ([MARA week 29](https://www.agri.cn/sj/jcyj/202607/t20260721_8856040.htm), 2026-07-20): winter vegetables 37 percent more than summer ones. Inside that week, cucumber moved 24.5 percent, spinach 11.0, oilseed rape greens 9.5 and lettuce 8.2.

**Nothing is posted at the stall.** State Administration for Market Regulation Order No. 56, in force since 2022-07-01, requires sellers to label prices accurately, keep the tag with the goods and make the marking conspicuous, while letting city-level regulators exempt specific trades from marking ([full text](https://scjgj.cq.gov.cn/zz/ddkq/zfxxgkml/fbzdjz/202302/t20230214_11603991.html), issued 2022). I have no measurement of wet-market compliance and will not invent one. A national market price for pork exists because the ministry samples 500 county markets, not because the markets publish anything.

**Policy moves shelf prices.** Imported beef has been under a safeguard measure since 2026-01-01: country quotas, then an extra 55 percent on the applied tariff once a country's quota is used ([MOFCOM Announcement 2025 No. 87](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2026/art_eb20ffdc45374e469571312d1498beda.html), dated 2025-12-31). Australia reached 100 percent of its quota on 2026-06-18 and the extra tariff applied from 00:00 on 2026-06-20 ([Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/20260619/07eb0fab463941dcb314cf195f04c7b5/c.html), 2026-06-19). Fruit goes the other way: Thai Monthong durian started at 28 yuan per kilo at a wholesale market in Yunnan, a decline Xinhua attributes to cold-chain rail freight ([Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260508/7ef22c7c8359491782f62fa5888426d7/c.html), 2026-05-08, which prices that 28 yuan at 4.09 US dollars — about 14.6 US cents to the yuan).

**And prices travel.** The 500-county series had pork at 23.00 yuan per kilo on 2026-01-08 ([MARA](https://xmsyj.moa.gov.cn/jcyj/202601/t20260113_6480632.htm), 2026-01-13) and 20.96 on 2026-07-23 ([week 4 of July](https://xmsyj.moa.gov.cn/jcyj/202607/t20260729_6486285.htm), 2026-07-29), down 8.9 percent. Wholesale pork went from 17.44 in the week of 22–28 December 2025 ([Xinhua](http://www.news.cn/food/20260106/b1c09adc0f2a40389df51c51db781034/c.html), 2026-01-06) to 15.89 in the week of 13–19 July 2026 ([MARA week 29](https://www.agri.cn/sj/jcyj/202607/t20260721_8856040.htm), 2026-07-20), also down 8.9 percent. Wholesale eggs in that July week were 42.3 percent higher year on year. Any figure you read for Chinese food prices has a shelf life of about a season.

## Where these numbers come from

Primary series wherever one exists, keeping the sources' own units and dates rather than harmonising them.

- **Wholesale:** the ministry's daily index bulletin, printing pork, beef, lamb, egg, vegetable and fruit averages as of 14:00 each day from the national wholesale price information system ([2026-07-24](http://scs.moa.gov.cn/jcyj/202607/t20260724_6486145.htm)), plus its weekly reports ([week 29](https://www.agri.cn/sj/jcyj/202607/t20260721_8856040.htm)).
- **Farm market:** the same ministry's 500-county weekly series, each report naming its collection day ([January 2026](https://xmsyj.moa.gov.cn/jcyj/202601/t20260113_6480632.htm)).
- **Retail by channel:** city development and reform commission monthly bulletins — [February 2026](https://fgw.beijing.gov.cn/gzdt/fgzs/gzdt/202603/t20260317_4559408.htm) for the channel columns, a second city's unlabelled averages ([May 2026](https://fgw.shenyang.gov.cn/wjgz/clzxxfbpt/fxyc/202606/t20260601_5034803.html)) for the last one. None of those cities is where I live, and I am not naming the province or city where I buy food.
- **Cross-checks:** the 36-city centre average, pork at 13.19 yuan per 500 grams on 2026-07-08 ([China Price Information Network](http://www.chinaprice.cn/spscdt/60726.jhtml), 2026-07-09); the statistics bureau's July 2026 CPI note ([NBS](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202608/t20260809_1965009.html)); the FAO Food Price Index, 136.0 points in September 2026 ([FAO](https://www.fao.org/worldfoodsituation/foodpricesindex/en/), 2026-10-02).
- **Platform fees:** trade-press reports of the platforms' own app notices ([packaging](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608193846383530.html), [delivery and pickup](https://stcn.com/article/detail/3822368.html)).
- **Crowd-sourced:** Numbeo (updated October 2026) and LivingCost, food at 235 US dollars a month for one person ([LivingCost](https://livingcost.org/cost/china), dated 2026-06-21 on the page).

The kilogram conversions, basket totals and percentage gaps are my arithmetic, labelled where they appear.

## What I could not check

- **Warehouse club prices.** No public item-price series exists, so that channel is a membership fee and nothing else — reported by news and trade outlets quoting the companies, not by the retailers' own pages, which would not load.
- **Delivery item prices and fee notices.** No public series: the delivery channel is fees and thresholds taken from trade press quoting app notices, not from a rate card.
- **Imported beef prices.** Removed rather than kept: the only item-level figure I found was a news survey dated June 2024, before the 2026 safeguard.
- **Wet-market price-tag compliance.** The rule requires tags; compliance is what I could not measure.
- **The card fee and the exchange rate.** The 3 percent fee rests on secondary carriers of the companies' statements, and the yuan rate on one news report's rounding of a single price.
- **The vegetable and fruit rows.** The vegetable farm-market figure is a 20-variety average whose channel the source never names; the fruit retail figure is a contributor-reported average set against an official wholesale series from another month.
- **LivingCost's food figure and the cheese price.** One aggregated number with no visible method, and a crowd-sourced item labelled "local cheese" that may mean domestically produced.
- **Early markets as a monitored category.** No series separates a pre-dawn market from the permanent one; that channel is the wet-market price with a different closing time.
- **Anything about my own city.** Deliberately absent.
